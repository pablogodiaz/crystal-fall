Crystal Fall

Every quarantined city hides a Crystal. Find it, shatter it, survive while it holds the city, and take the road to the next city. Downtown is crowded and well stocked, the outskirts quiet and bare. The dead hear gunshots and running feet, but lose you if you slip away. One life per run.
